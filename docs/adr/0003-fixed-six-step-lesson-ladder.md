# A unit is a fixed six-step lesson ladder

Every unit is authored as the same ordered ladder of lesson types: `Intro → BruteForce → Insight → Optimize → Tradeoff → Recognize`. This fixed template maps directly onto the three things a completed unit must prove (can solve it; understands the brute-force→optimized evolution and its tradeoffs; can recognize sibling problems), and gives the pipeline a stable structure to fill and the app a stable sequence to render.

## Consequences

The pipeline authors against a known shape rather than inventing a per-problem structure, at the cost of forcing every problem into the same arc; problems that don't fit the brute-force→optimized narrative may need this ADR revisited.
